02

Service Description

GOBLIN CARD provides Goblin Physical, a non-KYC Mastercard crypto debit card that allows users to deposit multiple cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH, SOL, XMR, USDT) and spend in fiat currencies worldwide. Goblin Physical holds its balance in Mexican pesos (MXN). Every Goblin Physical holder can get Goblin Virtual, including existing customers. These are two separate cards. Goblin Virtual is a Visa with a balance in US dollars (USD) that supports Apple Pay, Google Pay, and 3D Secure (3DS).

Publicly sharing a card BIN will result in indefinite suspension of your account with no refund, including where the card has not yet been successfully used, except where a refund is required by applicable law.

07

Refunds

Card-order refunds are available for any reason before successful card use, subject to the BIN-sharing exception above. Before shipping, eligible orders receive a full refund. After shipping, eligible refunds exclude the shipping fee, which is deducted from the refund amount.

Once the card has been successfully used for a transaction, the card order is no longer eligible for a refund. This policy concerns the purchase of your card, not refunds from merchants for purchases made using the card.

To request a refund, contact support with your order details. These conditions do not limit any refund rights required by applicable law.

08

Payment and Fees

The current Goblin Physical and Goblin Virtual offer costs $350 USD as a one-time payment, payable in Monero (XMR), USDT, or Bitcoin (BTC). Our current $350 USD offer gets you two separate cards for the price of one: Goblin Physical and Goblin Virtual. After full release, the two cards will be sold separately.

Goblin Physical: deposits are charged at 3.5%, or 4% for XMR. There are no FX fees (0%), no Goblin Card ATM withdrawal fees (0%), and no monthly charges. ATM operators may charge their own fees.

Goblin Virtual: approximately 1.5% FX compared with rates shown on Google and similar services. This includes a 1% markup on the underlying conversion rate; the exact difference varies by currency and conversion time. Cash withdrawals: Not currently available. Goblin Physical fee terms do not describe Goblin Virtual.

09

Transaction Limits

Goblin Physical has a spending limit of $2,500 USD per transaction, a daily spending limit of $5,000 USD, and a daily ATM withdrawal limit of $500 USD. There is no monthly spending limit. Goblin Virtual limits: Tap-to-pay: USD $20,000 per transaction and USD $20,000 per day. Online payments: USD $200,000 per transaction and USD $200,000 per day. Both the per-transaction and daily limits apply. We reserve the right to adjust these limits at our discretion.
