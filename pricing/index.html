Fees, explained.

Goblin Physical deposits: 3.5% · XMR: 4% · Minimum: $100 USD

What are Goblin Physical’s fees?

Goblin Physical deposits: 3.5% for BTC, ETH, SOL, and USDT; 4% for XMR. Minimum deposit is $100. Goblin Physical has 0% FX fees, 0% Goblin Card ATM fees, and no monthly fee. ATM operators may charge their own fees.

What are Goblin Virtual’s fees and limits?

The Goblin Virtual holds USD. Its advertised 1.5% FX represents the approximate total difference compared with exchange rates shown on Google and similar services. A 1% FX markup is added on top of the underlying conversion rate, which can already differ from those reference rates. The 1% is not applied directly to the rate you see on Google, and it is included in the roughly 1.5% overall difference, not charged on top of it. The exact difference varies by currency and conversion time. Tap-to-pay: USD $20,000 per transaction and USD $20,000 per day. Online payments: USD $200,000 per transaction and USD $200,000 per day. Both limits apply: a payment must be within the single-transaction limit and your remaining daily allowance. Splitting payments does not increase the daily limit.

Can I order the cards separately, or get Goblin Virtual on its own?

No. We do not offer Goblin Physical or Goblin Virtual separately at the moment. Goblin Virtual, our Visa card, is issued to existing, trusted Goblin users and cannot be purchased on its own.
