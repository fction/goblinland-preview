Your balance reflects the crypto payment we receive, less the applicable coin and product fees—not the amount you originally entered. For Goblin Physical: BTC, ETH, SOL, XMR, or USDT. Use the web portal (recommended) or the Telegram bot, and follow the displayed wallet address and network instructions.

Our current $350 USD offer gets you two separate cards for the price of one: Goblin Physical and Goblin Virtual. International trackable shipping, activation, and support are included. After full release, the two cards will be sold separately.

Goblin Physical: 0% FX and 0% Goblin ATM fees, with a 3.5% deposit fee (4% for XMR) and a $100 USD minimum deposit. Goblin Virtual: approximately 1.5% FX compared with rates shown on Google and similar services. This includes a 1% FX markup added on top of the underlying conversion rate, not directly to the rate shown on Google. The exact difference varies by currency and conversion time. A 2% cash withdrawal fee applies separately. ATM operators may apply their own charges. See Pricing for full details.
