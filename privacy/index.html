01

Introduction

GOBLIN CARD, operated by Goblin Solutions LLC ("we", "our", or "us"), is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our crypto debit card service.

02

Information We Collect

The physical card does not require identity documents. This does not mean that no data is processed when you use the website or card service. We only retain data that our card issuing partners and payment providers require us to hold in order to operate the service. This may include:

  • Transaction data, including amounts, timestamps, and merchant information
  • Cryptocurrency wallet addresses used for refills
  • Delivery and contact details supplied when ordering a physical card
  • Communication records when you contact our support team
  • Technical data such as device information and IP addresses

03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process transactions and maintain your card balance
  • Provide customer support and respond to your queries
  • Detect and prevent fraud and unauthorized activities
  • Improve our services and develop new features
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations, and with requirements set by our providers and partners

04

Data Minimization

We do not collect or store data beyond what is required for the service to function and what our providers and partners mandate for compliance purposes. We do not sell your data or share it beyond what's necessary to operate the card and meet these requirements.

05

Website Analytics and External Services

This website uses Plausible Analytics to measure website usage. Ordering and card management take place on our separate web portal. Following links to Telegram, SimpleX, X, or other external services makes those services' privacy policies applicable to your use of them. See the Plausible data policy for information about its analytics data processing.

06

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

07

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Revised" date and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible.
